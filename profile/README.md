# Rayer Group

**One operator. Companies built on AI systems.**

Rayer Group is the holding layer: it owns the pipelines — research, matching, delivery — and every venture runs on them. The same playbook is available to clients.

---

## Ventures

- **[RATIO Advisory](https://ratioadvisory.it)** — AI-driven scouting of grants and subsidised finance for Italian SMEs.
  700–800 funding calls analysed every year · 150+ regulatory sources monitored daily.

## How we work

- **AI where the work repeats.** Monitoring, reading and matching run on automated pipelines.
- **Humans where judgement matters.** The calls that carry weight stay with a person: human-in-the-loop by design, not as a safety net.
- **Compliance built in.** Systems are designed around the EU AI Act's principles of transparency and human oversight, and client data is handled in line with GDPR.
- **Build once, reuse everywhere.** Systems live at holding level; each venture runs on them.

One-man company by design: nothing to scale but the systems.
